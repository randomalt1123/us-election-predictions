Methodology

Each poll is converted to a two-party Democratic share, then to a D−R margin.
Polls are weighted by √(sample size) and an exponential recency factor with a 30-day half-life. The weighted average is then used as the generic ballot estimate. In "Shift from 2024" mode on the map, every district is shifted by the difference between this average's margin and the 2024 national House margin (R+2.0).
