Methodology

Each poll is weighted by recency (Gaussian kernel, 7-day bandwidth) and sample size (√n). The smoothed trend line uses the same kernel across 100 evenly-spaced time points. The displayed average always matches the rightmost point of the trend line. In Shift from 2024 mode on the map, every district is shifted by the difference between this average's D−R margin and the 2024 national House baseline.

For simplicity, I include the same polls as VoteHub, excluding very low quality pollsters and outliers. Currently, polls are not weighted by pollster rating, but I am working on an update.
